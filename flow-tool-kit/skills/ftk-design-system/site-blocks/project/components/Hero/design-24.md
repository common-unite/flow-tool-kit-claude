# Hero 24: Coral gradient split

**Use it when:** A section that must feel different from the rest of a navy and green site (a problem, a warning, another audience).

**Why it works:** A coral gradient ground with an illustration and three points as white cards changes the mood without breaking the system.

**Content it needs:** one photo or screenshot; 3 bullets; a heading of about 12 words. If the client cannot supply it, pick another design.
