# Hero 25: Mint grid with a mascot

**Use it when:** A closing or reassurance section near the end of a services page.

**Why it works:** A mint ground with a grid texture and the just-right bear says calm and finished; two buttons give the next step.

**Content it needs:** a mascot or cut-out illustration; 2 buttons; a heading of about 8 words. If the client cannot supply it, pick another design.
