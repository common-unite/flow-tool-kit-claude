# Cards 03: Questions on diagonal navy

**Use it when:** Three short questions and answers (a mini FAQ) on a dark section.

**Why it works:** Clean cards have no box, so the questions read as text on the diagonal gradient and stay the focus.

**Content it needs:** 3 items. If the client cannot supply it, pick another design.

| Prop | Property Editor | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `background` | Background: Background | `#032D60` |
| `backgroundGradient` | Background: Gradient | `diagonal` (Diagonal) |
| `bodyAlign` | Body: Alignment | `left` (Left) |
| `brandColor` | Background: Brand Color | `#2E844A` |
| `cardStyle` | Bullets: Card Style | `clean` (Clean) |
| `hoverStyle` | Motion: Hover | `lift` (Lift) |

JSON (the Configure Properties value):

```json
{"sectionType": "cards", "brandColor": "#2E844A", "bodyAlign": "left", "columns": "3", "cardStyle": "clean", "items": [{"title": "Can I bring my kids?", "description": "Yes, ages 14 and up. Volunteers under 16 need an adult signed up for the same shift."}, {"title": "What if I have to cancel?", "description": "Cancel from your confirmation email any time. Twenty four hours notice helps us fill the spot."}, {"title": "Do you sign service hour forms?", "description": "Yes. Bring your form to check-in, or request a signed letter from your volunteer profile."}], "hoverStyle": "lift", "backgroundGradient": "diagonal", "background": "#032D60"}
```
